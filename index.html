Week 1

How to play

Five things. Then it's just you and your opinions.

  1. Pick a winner Tap either team. Their color floods that side of the card and the loser fades out. Changed your mind? Tap the same side again.
  2. Rank it 1 to 16 Your number 1 ranking is the game you'd bet the house on. A correct number 1 earns 16 points, number 2 earns 15, and so on down to number 16, which earns 1. Each number gets used exactly once, so choose carefully.
  3. Beat the kickoff Each game slams shut the moment it starts. Never picked it? That's a zero. No late entries, no exceptions, no sympathy.
  4. Call the tiebreaker Bottom of the Picks tab: combined points in the last game of the week, both teams. Usually Monday night in the regular season. Only comes out if two of you finish dead level.
  5. Watch the Grid Your own sheet is always on the Picks tab, editable right up to each kickoff. The Grid tab lines everyone up side by side. It stays sealed until a game starts, then fills in one column at a time all afternoon. Green means you called it.

What each rank pays

Bye weeks mean some weeks have fewer than sixteen games. The ranks and the payouts shrink with the slate, so a perfect week is worth less in a bye week. Everyone is on the same slate, so it evens out.

The line is only there to help you

You are picking straight-up winners, not against the spread. If a team wins by one point they won, and your pick was right.

The number on each card (KC -3) is Vegas telling you who they think wins and by how much. It is context for deciding your ranks. It has nothing to do with scoring, and it never affects your points.

Games that are not priced yet show line TBD. Books only post a week or so ahead.

How the pool picked

Once a game kicks off, every locked card shows the split: what percentage of the pool took each side. The Grid carries a The pool row along the bottom with the majority pick on every game.

It only ever appears after kickoff. Before that it would tell you what everyone else did, which is the whole thing we are protecting.

When the crowd goes down together the card gets a red Pool got it wrong tag. Those are the weeks that shake up a table, because whoever went against it just gained on everybody at once.

A tie is a tie

NFL games can end in a draw. When that happens, nobody scores points that game, whichever team you took. Rare, but it happens roughly once a season.

Badges

Post the highest score of anybody in the pool for a week and this is yours. Rack them up and it starts keeping count. Finish level with somebody and you both take one, no argument.

Second best that week. Close, but somebody was closer.

A perfect week. Read on.

Sit on top of the season table and your whole row turns gold with a Leader tag on it, in Standings and on the Grid. It is not a trophy, it is a seat, and somebody is always coming for it. Every time a game goes final the table re-sorts underneath you and a small arrow marks who just climbed or slid.

The Week by week panel in Standings keeps the receipts: who took every week, and what it cost them.

The perfect week

Call all sixteen games in a single week and a gold trophy goes next to your name in Standings, permanently. Do it twice and it carries a number.

Fair warning: flipping coins, your odds are about 1 in 65,000. Knowing ball helps, but not that much. Most seasons nobody gets one.

Strategy, briefly

Anyone can go 11-5. The question is which eleven. Same record, same games, and you might walk away with 100 points or 60. It all comes down to what you put your big numbers on.

Which is why nobody's ever really out of it. Thirty points back with four weeks left is one gutsy Sunday from the lead.

Your picks save themselves

Every tap saves instantly. Bail out halfway through, pick it back up on a different phone, and you're right where you left it.

Lock in is not a save button. It confirms the week is complete: every game picked, every rank assigned, tiebreaker entered.

Signing in

Enter your email, we send a six-digit code, you type it in. No password to forget. You stay signed in on that device, so you will not do this every week.

Your picks live with your account, not your phone. Sign in anywhere and they follow you.

Alerts

You only ever hear about games you have not picked. Finish the week early and it stays quiet. Turn categories on and off in Settings.

On iPhone, alerts only work if the app is on your Home Screen. That is an Apple rule, not ours.

Scoring

Straight-up gives 1 point per correct pick. Confidence has you rank all 16 games: rank 1 is your surest and pays 16 points, rank 16 is your coin flip and pays 1. A perfect week is 136.
